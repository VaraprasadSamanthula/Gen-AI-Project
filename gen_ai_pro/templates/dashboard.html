How It Works

Our Spam Detector uses advanced AI technology to analyze messages and identify spam content. Simply paste or type any message (email, SMS, or chat) into the text box below, and our dual-model system will instantly analyze it using:

  • TF-IDF Vectorization: Advanced text analysis that weights important words and phrases
  • LinearSVM Model: Machine learning classifier trained on balanced dataset
  • N-gram Analysis: Detects spam patterns using both single words and word pairs

  • ✅ SMS Filtering: Identify spam text messages and promotional scams
  • ✅ Chat Security: Protect yourself from malicious links and suspicious messages
  • ✅ Content Moderation: Filter unwanted content in messaging platforms

  • ✅ Business Use: Protect your organization from spam and phishing attacks
